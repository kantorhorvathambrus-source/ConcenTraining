Three drafts for a new app icon in the Night Lamp colours, with the current icon for comparison. Same idea in all three: a crosshair with a brain seen from above in the middle (two hemispheres that meet at the front and back, a narrow central fissure, three folds per side).

Draft only: nothing here is used by the app. At favicon size (16–32 px) the brain doesn't read, so the favicons keep the crosshair with a dot, in each variant's colours.
