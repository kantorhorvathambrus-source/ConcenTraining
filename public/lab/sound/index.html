Sound lab

Temporary page for picking the Focus sound variants. Each plays at the app's default volume (layer 70%, Volume 55%), through the same reverb and limiter as the app.

Listen to each one once on the phone speaker and once with headphones: phone speakers lose most sound below about 300 Hz, so the deepest part of Thunder, Train and Airplane is only there on headphones (the speaker gets its "shadow" instead).

Thunder: the first roll comes within 3–10 s, then one every 20–90 s. Listen to it for about 2 minutes.
