About ConcenTraining

ConcenTraining is a focus timer with characters. Pick a character, set your time and start a session. When you notice your mind has wandered, tap a button and your character reacts with a pose and a facial expression, never with words.

What you can do

  • Choose a character with the personality you need: relaxed and encouraging, firm, strict, or ragebait characters that annoy you on purpose with mocking poses.
  • Pick a mode: in Simple you tap how distracted you were; in Advanced you log how long, and see your focus % at the end.
  • Focus in a single block, or build a plan of several focus blocks with planned breaks in between. Pick a common length with one tap, or set any length to the minute (up to 4 hours of focus, breaks up to 2 hours).
  • Take a break when you need one: the timer waits, and the break doesn't count against your focus.
  • Play a steady brown, pink or white noise during focus time to cover distracting sounds. It's made right in your browser, so nothing is downloaded, and it fades out for every break.
  • Create your own character: pick the look, the personality and a name. For 10 minutes after saving you can still change everything; after that, only the name.
  • See your past sessions, streaks and focus trends on the History page, and export them as a backup.
  • Use it in 9 languages: English, Hungarian, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Italian, Indonesian and Polish.
  • It works in the browser on your phone or computer, and offline once you've opened it. No account needed.

Your data

Your sessions, history, settings and the characters you create stay on your device. When you start a session, the app sends only the name of the character you chose to our server (“custom” for one you created), so we can count which characters are popular.
