How it works

  • Pick a character and a timer, then start focusing.
  • When you notice your mind has wandered, tap how much (Simple) or how long (Advanced). Your character reacts with a pose, never with words. Noticing and coming back is the skill you're training.
  • If you leave the app during a session, it asks when you're back whether you were distracted.
  • Your history is saved automatically on this device. To move it to another phone, use Export on the history page →

Keep your history safe on iPhone

Safari may erase your history and streak if you don't open ConcenTraining for 7 days. Apps on your Home Screen keep it.

How: tap Share (or ••• → Share), then Add to Home Screen.

Important: the Home Screen app has its own separate storage, so your current history won't move over by itself. Export your history first, then Import it in the Home Screen app. Go to Export →
